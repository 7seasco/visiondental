Our Philosophy

Where science meets artistry

We opened on Santa Monica Boulevard in 2008 with a quiet conviction: that dental care could feel less like a transaction and more like a craft. Every detail — from the materials we choose, to the natural light that fills our rooms, to the way we greet you at the door — is shaped by that belief.

Fifteen years later, the same care still guides every appointment. We are proud to be a fixture of West LA, and to count multiple generations of families among our patients.

Good to know

Questions,
answered.

If we've missed yours, please call. Our concierge line is answered by a real member of our team — usually on the first ring.

Are you accepting new patients?
Yes. New-patient appointments include a comprehensive exam, a thorough cleaning, and time to discuss any concerns — with no pressure to commit to follow-up care.
Which insurance plans do you work with?
We accept most major dental insurance, including Delta Dental, MetLife, Cigna, Aetna, and Guardian. If your plan isn't listed, our front desk can walk you through your benefits and out-of-network options.
Do you offer financing or payment plans?
We offer flexible in-house plans and partner with CareCredit for longer-term financing. The full cost of any treatment is shared with you in advance — no surprises, no add-ons.
I'm anxious about dental visits. How do you accommodate that?
Often. We have extensive experience with dental anxiety and offer multiple sedation options. The chair adjusts, the lights soften, and we move at your pace — not ours.
How do I reserve my first appointment?
Call us at (213) 555-0100 or use the booking form. We'll send a brief intake ahead of time so the visit itself can focus on you, not paperwork.
