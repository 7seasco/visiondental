Good to know

Questions, answered.

If we missed yours, give us a call. A real human will pick up.

Do you accept new patients?
Yes — we'd love to meet you. New-patient visits include a full exam, a thorough cleaning, and time to talk through anything that's on your mind. No pressure on follow-up treatment.
What insurance do you accept?
We work with most major dental insurance plans including Delta Dental, MetLife, Cigna, Aetna, and Guardian. If your plan isn't listed, we'll still help you understand your benefits.
Do you offer payment plans?
We offer flexible in-house financing and partner with CareCredit for longer-term plans. We'll always walk you through the full cost up front — no surprises.
I'm anxious about dental visits. Can you help?
Absolutely. We have years of experience working with anxious patients and offer multiple sedation options. The chair adjusts, the lights dim, and we go at your pace.
How do I book my first visit?
Call us at (213) 555-0100 or use our online form. We'll send a short intake before your appointment so the visit itself can focus on you, not paperwork.
