Support

Questions

Answers to the most common things our patients ask about.

What should I expect at my first appointment?
Your first visit includes a comprehensive oral exam, digital X-rays, and a professional cleaning. We will take the time to discuss your dental history, address any concerns, and build a personalized treatment plan. Most first appointments last about 60 to 90 minutes.
Do you accept dental insurance?
Yes, we accept most major dental insurance plans including Delta Dental, Cigna, Aetna, MetLife, and Guardian. Our front desk team will verify your benefits before your appointment and help you understand your coverage so there are no surprises.
How often should I visit the dentist?
We recommend visiting every six months for routine check-ups and professional cleanings. Patients with gum disease, a history of cavities, or certain health conditions may benefit from more frequent visits. We will help you determine the best schedule based on your individual needs.
Is teeth whitening safe for my enamel?
Professional teeth whitening performed under dental supervision is safe for your enamel. We use carefully formulated gels and controlled application times to brighten your smile without damaging tooth structure. Some patients experience mild temporary sensitivity, which resolves quickly.
What are my options for replacing missing teeth?
We offer several solutions for missing teeth including dental implants, bridges, and partial dentures. Implants are the gold standard because they look, feel, and function like natural teeth while preserving jawbone health. During your consultation, we will recommend the best option for your situation and budget.
Do you offer payment plans for larger treatments?
Absolutely. We partner with CareCredit and Lending Club to offer flexible financing with low or zero-interest payment plans. We also accept HSA and FSA accounts. Our goal is to make quality dental care accessible without financial stress.
