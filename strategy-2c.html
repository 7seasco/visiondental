SUPPORT

Questions

What should I expect during my first visit?
Your first appointment includes a comprehensive oral examination, digital X-rays, and a personalized consultation. We take the time to understand your goals, review your dental history, and develop a tailored treatment plan -- all in a calm, unhurried environment.
Do you accept dental insurance?
We accept most major dental insurance plans and will work with your provider to maximize your benefits. Our team handles all claim submissions on your behalf, ensuring a seamless billing experience so you can focus on your care.
How often should I schedule a cleaning?
For most patients, we recommend professional cleanings every six months. Depending on your oral health profile, our team may suggest more frequent visits to address specific concerns such as gum health or buildup prevention.
What cosmetic procedures do you offer?
Our cosmetic suite includes professional whitening, porcelain veneers, dental bonding, gum contouring, and full smile makeovers. Each treatment is planned with digital previews so you can see your potential results before we begin.
Is sedation available for anxious patients?
Absolutely. We offer nitrous oxide and oral conscious sedation options to ensure your comfort during any procedure. Our team will discuss the best approach for your needs during your consultation.
How do I schedule an appointment?
You can book online through our scheduling system, call us directly, or use the Reserve button on this page. We offer flexible hours including early morning and late afternoon appointments to accommodate your schedule.
