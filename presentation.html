Quick wins

Long-tail keywords like "dental veneers cost" and "dental anxiety friendly" have low competition and high conversion intent.

Content strategy

Blog topics around care tips, procedure guides, and patient stories build authority and capture informational traffic.

Local SEO

Google Business Profile, local citations, and location schema markup. Multi-location strategy for both sites.
